# Why rebuild Resolution Pal

Research recorded November 1, 2026. Historical observations are evidence about the original product; they are not claims about the new deployment.

## Original intent and implementation

Dylan built Resolution Pal during parental leave as a hands-on experiment with AI-assisted development, inspired by his enthusiasm for New Year's resolutions. The original messaging was personal, playful, and welcoming. Its guided, Typeform-inspired interview aimed to learn someone's motivation, habits, constraints, and available time before offering a detailed annual plan.

The production source was Flask with vanilla JavaScript, OpenAI Assistants, and Render hosting. A React prototype also existed in the repository but was not the deployed application. The long adaptive interview, fragile deployment setup, limited recovery, and lack of save/resume made completing a useful plan harder than the idea warranted. See the [original source history](https://github.com/dylsell/resolutionpal/commit/878bfb4).

At the start of this rebuild, the existing domain stalled at Render's waking screen. This was consistent with earlier service-failure evidence, but the exact live environment still needed authenticated inspection. The old application should be preserved as source history, not used as a dependable functional rollback.

## Promotion and evidence

- The [December 2024 LinkedIn launch](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dylsell_i-have-always-loved-new-years-resolutions-activity-7272330156088688640-GrD2) led with Dylan's personal interest in resolutions and included a demo. It reached 3,304 impressions in the inspected post analytics.
- Original planning material also drafted an explanation of how AI tools helped build the product for a largely nontechnical audience. A draft is evidence of intended messaging, not proof that a second post was published.
- The [February 2026 rebuild post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7430619103037386752) revisited the project. Inspected analytics reported 98 website visits. Its edit and comments confirm the domain was still pointing at the old broken site.
- Searches did not locate the newer implementation mentioned in that post. Nor did the inspected material establish paid promotion, a mailing-list campaign, monetization, or reliable historical product conversion. These remain unknown rather than evidence that they never existed.

## What carries forward

Keep the personal origin, approachable mascot, and thoughtful reflection. Make one useful resolution the unit of work. Replace a long annual interview with four setup screens and two tailored questions, then deliver first-week actions, a sustainable weekly routine, and 30/60/90-day milestones. Save progress locally and make keeping the result straightforward.

Use the channel that has demonstrably reached people: Dylan's LinkedIn network, supported by a brief product demo. A relaunch should follow a completed production journey on the real domain. Do not repeat the gap between announcing a rebuild and routing people to a working experience.

## LinkedIn draft

I still love New Year's resolutions. I also know how easy it is to make one that doesn't fit your actual life.

Two years ago, while on parental leave, I built Resolution Pal to help with that. It was a fun way to learn what I could make with AI. It was also slow, unfinished, and eventually pretty broken.

I've rebuilt it.

You bring one thing you'd like to change. Resolution Pal asks a few questions about why it matters, what's getting in the way, and the time you really have. Then it helps you turn that into a first week you can start, a weekly routine, and a 90-day path—with a smaller version for the weeks when life gets busy.

It's free, there's no signup, and you can keep your plan or save it as a PDF. You can start today; January 1 is optional.

Try it: https://resolutionpal.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=linkedin-launch

I'd love to hear whether the plan feels realistic for your life—and where it misses.
